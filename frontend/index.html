ECG BASICS

Reading the heart's electrical story

An electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG) records the heart's electrical signals through small electrodes placed on the skin. Clinicians use ECGs to examine heart rate and rhythm and help assess a range of heart conditions.

This focuses on short, numerical heartbeat segments. It offers a hands-on way to see how a trained model can turn signal data into a classification result.
